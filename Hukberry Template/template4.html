Colder weather begs one important question: How many jackets are too many jackets? Around the office, most of us would say there’s no such thing—but, to be pragmatic, all you truly need is three. If we had to rebuild our wardrobes from the ground up, we’d start with the essentials: a classic waxed jacket that looks good, rain or shine; an insulated layer built for reliable protection against the cold; and an unstructured blazer that strikes the right balance between casual and refined. Few brands can say they make their shoes entirely by hand. Even fewer can say they’ve been doing it for over 200 years. Founded in 1820 and run by the same family for seven generations, Astorflex crafts every pair in its Northern Italian workshop, using natural materials sourced close to home. The Bootflex—just restocked in a new Burnished Grey Suede color—exemplifies the brand’s longstanding attention to detail. It boasts a rugged bovine leather upper that’ll develop a rich patina with wear, a rubber sole for reliable support, and memory foam insoles that offer far more comfort than your standard boot.
