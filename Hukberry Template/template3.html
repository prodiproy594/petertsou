Ivy and workwear initially sat on opposite ends of the spectrum, but, with time, they found common ground, giving and taking as the American style identity took shape. Our bestselling Boiled Wool Chore Blazer, which is back in new colors for fall, nods to this history. It’s crafted from fine wool that’s twice-boiled to tighten the fibers—a centuries-old process that improves wind and water resistance without the bulk. The result is an unstructured, weather-resistant layer that’s utilitarian enough for daily duty. With functional buttons that extend all the way to the neck and a trio of front pockets, you can wear it lapel-down like a classic blazer or lapel-up like a chore coat—it strikes the perfect balance between both influences.

In the 1970s, a ragtag guerrilla climbing group called The Stonemasters scaled Yosemite’s steep cliffs and sheer rock faces in bright white canvas painter pants. Although technical mountaineering gear was widely available, they proved granite demands no-nonsense durability and, in doing so, took the sport to new heights. Our designers behind Proof honored this history (and their spirit) with the Rover Double-Knee Work Pant. Built from the Rover Collection’s signature stretch canvas fabric, which resists abrasions, wicks moisture, and regulates your temperature, it features a gusseted crotch and dart seams behind both knees for better range of motion and six pockets for all your EDC—perfect for daily wear, not just daring adventures.

For three generations, the Palomos have quietly helmed one of the best-kept secrets in handcrafted footwear. The Salvadoran family made smart moves early on by insisting on owning the entire production process—from the tannery that treats their hides and the factory that makes their soles to the studio where each pair gets assembled. Over 70 years later, they’re distilling decades of experience into Palomo 1953, which has quickly grown into an internationally recognized name for hand-sewn, heritage-inspired, Goodyear-welted boots and loafers made from the world’s finest leathers, without the markup.
