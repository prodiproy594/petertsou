Like every piece from Relwen, the Quilted Flannel Shirt Jacket stems from founder Jamie Rupp’s studies of the past. He set out to create his own version of the vintage shirt jackets from his archive, trimming the bulk without sacrificing what makes the style a reliable fall essential. He achieved this by cutting and sewing the 100% cotton brushed flannel shell and quilted ripstop lining together as one piece. This technique, along with 80g of down-alternative insulation, results in a shirt jacket that’s as lightweight as it is warm. Our pick from the new fall patterns is this custom duck camo based on our Director of Merchandising’s own vintage flannel, which caught Jamie’s eye on a visit to our HQ last year.

For over a century, Red Wing has been crafting hardwearing footwear for conditions across the globe. New from the Minnesota-based brand is the Norway Moc Boot 8213, a modern interpretation of the original boot built to handle Norway’s tough terrain. The boot, made in the USA at the Red Wing headquarters outside Minneapolis, takes scotch-grain embossed Copper Rough & Tough leather and pairs it with a Vibram® Fire & Ice lug outsole. In proper fashion, we sent the boot to our friend, ecologist Charles Post, to test on his remote farm in Lofoten, Norway.
