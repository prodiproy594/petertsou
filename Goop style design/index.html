Why Your Airplane Window Seat Needs a 20-Second Reset

Did you know your phone screen carries up to 10 times more bacteria than most public surfaces—especially after passing through airport security trays? Yet wiping your phone or prescription glasses with alcohol wipes or paper towels gradually strips away the factory oleophobic (oil-repelling) coating.

The Smart Fix: Before takeoff, give your phone and eyewear a dry, 10-second sweep with an antimicrobial microfiber weave, and pair it with a glass of electrolyte-rich water to offset cabin dry air. Your eyes—and your camera roll—will thank you upon landing.
