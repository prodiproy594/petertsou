First developed in partnership with Kelly Air Force Basein 1986, the Marathon Navigator is one of the most recognizable military-issue watches ever designed. Its purpose-built asymmetrical case cushions the crown, a potentially life-saving feature that prevents it from snagging on parachute rigging, gear webbing, or cockpit equipment. To celebrate its longstanding legacy as a reliable toolfor pilots and paratroopers, we collaborated with Marathon on a custom version of the Pilot’s Navigator with a 41mm CeraShell® case, a new, proprietary material made from bio-sourced plastic and ceramic powder. Ours features the style’s first-ever Arctic dial, a radial-mounted tritium tube insert affixed to the bezel pip, a custom orange seconds hand, and a Huckberry-logo-engraved-crown.
 

Modeled after a well-worn vintage hunting jacket from our design director’s personal collection, Flint and Tinder’s new Dry Wax Field Jacket gives the time-tested silhouette new life. It’s cut from Ranger 8 dry-wax canvas sourced from British Millerain, the legendary English mill founded in 1880 that patented the first paraffin-waxed outerwear in 1894. The refined jacket, complete with Moleskin trim, a flannel blanket-lined body, and nylon-lined sleeves, repels water and develops a unique patina with wear. With six external pockets for stashing EDC and keeping your hands warm, it’s just as functional as the original jacket that inspired it, but much easier to dress up.
